What is a Healthcheck?

A health check offers a detailed overview of the Snow Software instance utilised by Wood Group UK Limited It covers information about the servers hosting the application, as well as the product's usage and settings. This data allows us to provide specific recommendations for improvements and enhance overall performance. Our goal is to ensure that our customers get the most use out of the product.

Recommendations & Advice

Priority Improvements Required:

Addressing immediate issues like outdated applications, security vulnerabilities, or non-functioning products.

• VMware vSphere needs further investigation to understand why its not reporting in correctly.
• 49 User accounts which haven’t accessed the platform in the past 3 months needs to be removed from the platform to enhance security.
• Most Snow Inventory agents are on an unsupported version, this should be rectified to ensure security patches have been applied and the agents continue to function correctly.
• Review of License compliance, including Under and Over Licensed to ensure everything has been displaying correctly.
• There are currently 4349 discovered devices which have been detected via AD discovery and/or SIM connectors but do not have a Snow agent installed. It is recommended to install the Snow agent on all devices in the estate to ensure the best quality of data and to ensure that all applications are monitored for compliance.

ENHANCED UTILISATION OPPORTUNITIES:

Identifying features or tools the customer isn't currently using but would benefit from.

• Custom Fields, Objects and Notifications are not currently utilized but could benefit Wood by allowing the inclusion of data that is not currently available within Snow.

BEST PRACTICE ADJUSTMENTS:

Suggesting minor changes to settings and usage to better adhere to best practices.
