What is a Build Sheet?

What is a Health Check?

{{Document_Introduction}}

A build sheet provides a complete record of the Snow configuration established for {{customer_name}}. It details the setup of the hosting servers, inventory agents, and platform settings as they were at the time of delivery. Our goal is to ensure you have a clear, documented implementation of the product.

A health check offers a detailed overview of the Snow Software instance utilised by {{customer_name}} It covers information about the servers hosting the application, as well as the product's usage and settings. This data allows us to provide specific recommendations for improvements and enhance overall performance. Our goal is to ensure that our customers get the most use out of the product.
